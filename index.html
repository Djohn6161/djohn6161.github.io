01 — About

Backend depth first — then everything around it.

I learned PHP the long way. At Biggs Inc. I built four database-driven internal systems — HRIS 201, PAM, a Performance Management System, and an employee training portal — in Vanilla PHP and MySQL, with no framework doing the work for me. That meant designing the normalized schemas, writing the query layer, and handling routing, auth, and validation by hand. It's the reason I can read an unfamiliar codebase quickly instead of waiting for the framework to explain it.

That paid off at Aleads, where I joined a 5-person remote engineering team working inside a custom in-house PHP framework. In my first six weeks I shipped 80+ production client sites and built 2 reusable templates other developers now extend. I lean on AI-assisted tooling (Claude, Lovable) to trace legacy conventions fast, and I connect the backend to what the business actually measures — GTM, GA4, and Search Console, configured and deployed myself.
